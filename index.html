    What this does not see
    

      
Frontier lab spending. Internal safety budgets at Anthropic, Google DeepMind, OpenAI and others are not disclosed by area and likely exceed philanthropy in interpretability, evaluations and alignment.

      
Venture capital. Investment in safety-adjacent startups sits behind paid databases.

      
Government money. UK AI Security Institute, NSF, EU programmes and similar are not yet included.

      
Private donors. Individual and family-office giving is not public. Manifund search returns 50 projects per query, so some older Manifund projects may be missing.

      
Note: a thin area is thin in public philanthropy terms, not necessarily underfunded nor thin overall.

    

  

  

    Methodology
    

      

      
Each grant gets one research area and one method, assigned by Claude from its title and description. General-support grants are tagged by the organisation's main focus and marked inferred. Unidentified organisations go to unclassified.

      
Grants between tracked funders (for example Coefficient Giving to EA Funds, SFF to Manifund) are excluded so money is counted once to avoid double counting. Multi-year grants count in full at award date, in nominal USD.
